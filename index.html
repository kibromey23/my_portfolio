Featured Work

CropGuard AI

Capstone thesis Computer vision Web platform 7-person team, lead developer

Smallholder farmers in Tigray have little accessible means to diagnose crop disease before it costs them a season's yield. CropGuard AI is a web-based platform that lets a farmer upload a photo of a leaf and receive an instant, confidence-scored diagnosis, in their own language.

I trained a MobileNetV2-based CNN on the full PlantVillage dataset, then built JWT-authenticated APIs, per-user scan history, quota enforcement for a free/premium model, and a trilingual interface (English, Amharic, Tigrinya; 280+ localized keys). The whole system runs on free-tier infrastructure end to end.
