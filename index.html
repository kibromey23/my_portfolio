Projects

Featured project

01 / Capstone thesis

CropGuard AI

Computer vision Web platform Seven-person capstone team

Problem

Smallholder farmers in Tigray have little accessible means to check a crop for disease before it costs a season's yield.

Solution

CropGuard AI is a web-based plant disease detection platform that lets a farmer upload a leaf image and receive a confidence-scored classification, in English, Amharic, or Tigrinya.

My role

Led the ML pipeline, backend architecture, and deployment. I trained a MobileNetV2-based CNN using the PlantVillage dataset, working with 87,867 training images across 38 disease classes, then built JWT-authenticated APIs, per-user scan history, quota enforcement for a free/premium model, and the trilingual interface (280+ localized keys).
