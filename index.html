01 · Featured case study

CropGuard AI

A deep-learning crop-disease platform spanning mobile, web, backend and ML services. Lead Developer & AI Engineer · 2025–2026.

94%validation accuracy
38disease classes
87,867PlantVillage images

Computer visionTransfer learningFull-stack AICapstone thesis

Turn a crop photo into an actionable disease assessment.

Problem. Smallholder farmers in Tigray can lack accessible tools for early crop-disease diagnosis.

Solution. CropGuard lets farmers photograph or upload a crop image and receive a confidence-scored diagnosis through a multilingual product experience, with scan history, quota controls and an agricultural AI assistant.

My contribution. Led the ML pipeline and contributed across backend architecture, authentication, deployment and product integration within a seven-person capstone team.

System architecture
React / Vite→Node / Express→FastAPI→MobileNetV2→PostgreSQL

ML inference is exposed through APIs and connected to the web/mobile product, persistence and deployment layer.

ML implementation

MobileNetV2-based CNN using the full PlantVillage dataset, with model evaluation using precision, recall, F1 and confusion-matrix methods.

Product engineering

JWT authentication, per-user scan history, free/premium quota enforcement, mobile Android build and cloud deployment.

Language & assistance

English, Amharic and Tigrinya interface plus an AI agricultural assistant powered through the Anthropic API.
